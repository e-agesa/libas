What you asked for, and where it stands

As before, every item was reproduced on the live system before being changed and checked again on the live system afterwards. The screenshots in this section are from libasulanwar.com.

What you told usWhat was doneStatus
"We have the same product in a lot of sizes. Variation is to be added to all products — we will choose where to keep variation and where not to."
Every product now has variations available, and each variation keeps its own stock, its own price and its own photograph. A product you do not want to vary simply keeps one, and behaves exactly as before. Your catalogue came through the change with 509 variations across 468 products.
 Fixed
"For each product, such as the white cap in size 21.5, can we have separate design options? We want multiple photos under one product because the designs are different."
Each variation carries its own photograph, and that photograph is what appears at the till and in the invoice picker — so Design 1 and Design 2 no longer look identical on screen. A demonstration product, Sample Topi — Variation Photos (demo), is on your system so you can see it working; delete it whenever you like.
 Fixed
"Variations on a product should last to the POS."
They do. The till lists each variation as its own tile with its own price and stock count, and selling one takes the units off that variation and no other. A size that has run out cannot be sold even while other sizes of the same product still have stock.
 Fixed
"I'm getting a constant error uploading an image for a product — it says the file should be less than 2 MB but it is not accepting even after resize. My stock entering is on hold."
The 2 MB rule was the fault: photographs from a phone arrive at 4–12 MB, so every one was refused. The limit is now 12 MB and the system shrinks the photo itself on the way in. Nothing needs resizing by hand.
 Fixed
"Whenever we add a product, the minimum quantity automatically defaults to 5. Could this default please be removed — the field should be left blank."
Removed; the field now starts blank. One clarification worth having: that box is the Minimum Stock Alert — it decides when a product is flagged as running low. It has never limited how many you may sell. Products created before this change may still show a 5, which you can clear on each one; clearing it now sticks.
 Fixed
"It is not displaying the code and colour when selecting the fabric. Please include decimal values in the quantity as well."
Choosing a fabric now reads "AM01 · AL MAS FABRIC — blue · cotton (KES 400)" — code, name, colour, type and price on one line. The fabric code is also its own column in the Fabrics module and searchable there. Quantities accept decimals, so 2.5 metres bills correctly. Off-the-shelf items stay whole units, since they are counted stock.
 Fixed
"They will be using a thermal printer — the system needs a print option for invoices in thermal format."
Every invoice has a thermal receipt sized for an 80mm roll, printable straight from the invoice. The till's receipt uses the same layout, so the counter prints one thing however the sale was rung up.
 Fixed
"Optimise the search on the line item — it only picks the first letter. They want to search by typing the full name, full code or colour."
Choosing an off-the-shelf item is now a search box, not a dropdown: type any part of the name, code, colour, size or design, in any order, and it narrows as you type. Note: the fabric chooser on a tailoring line is still an ordinary dropdown — it now shows the code and colour, but you cannot type into it. Say the word and we will make it a search box too.
 Fixed
"Deleting or voiding an invoice never returns its stock. It should return stock and all calculations done on it."
Voiding or deleting an invoice now puts the stock back, on the exact variation it came from, and writes it into the stock history so the change is not a mystery later. It can only happen once, so voiding and then deleting the same invoice cannot return the units twice. Note: invoices voided or deleted before this change did not return their stock and were not corrected retrospectively — see the open items below.
 Fixed
"Invoice sales are invisible in the stock movement log."
An off-the-shelf item sold on an invoice now appears in the stock movement log the same way a till sale does, naming the variation that left the shelf. Note: this covers off-the-shelf items. Fabric consumed by tailoring work is still not deducted or logged — see the open items below.
 Fixed
"Make the logo big on the receipt, enlarge the fonts, add the address and phone number at the bottom, and make space for a thank-you message."
All four. The receipt now leads with your logo, the type is a size up throughout, your address and telephone close it, and the thank-you stands on its own with clear roll beneath it for a handwritten note or a stamp. In finding this we discovered your logo had never printed on any document — see below.
 Fixed
"Edit option to be there on invoices."
Not done yet. An invoice's status can be changed, a payment recorded, a quotation turned into an invoice, and an unpaid invoice deleted — but the items on a saved invoice cannot yet be changed. Correcting a billed item today means deleting the invoice and entering it again, which uses up the invoice number. This is the largest of the three open items and we would like to agree the rules with you before building it.

Faults nobody had noticed

These were found while working on the items above. Two of them were losing your data quietly, which is the worst kind of fault: nothing appears to go wrong at the time.

What we foundWhy it matteredStatus
A product's own photograph was being destroyed by ordinary use of the Variations & Photos panel
Uploading a photograph for one variation replaced the product's own picture; deleting that photograph again erased it altogether. The file was never deleted, only the link to it — but the product would show a grey placeholder with no explanation. If any product lost its picture in the last few days, this is why. Those will need re-uploading; it cannot happen again.
 Fixed
Your logo had never printed on any PDF
The logo was uploaded and showing on screen, but every PDF — invoice, statement and receipt — was looking for the file in the wrong place, finding nothing, and silently printing no logo at all. It now prints on all of them.
 Fixed
Choosing a photograph as the "main" one did nothing
The choice was recorded but never used — the product kept leading with whichever photograph happened to be first. Now the one you choose is the one that shows.
 Fixed
The last unit could be sold twice
Two people billing the same last item at the same moment could both succeed, driving stock negative. Each sale now claims its units in a way the other cannot duplicate, on the invoice screen, at the till and on the website.
 Fixed
A payment could be lost when two people recorded one at once
A cashier settling an order at the till while somebody recorded a payment on the invoice screen could overwrite the other's figure — the payment stayed on record but vanished from the amount paid. Both routes are now taken one at a time.
 Fixed
Settling a pending order at the till lost the variation
Pulling a customer's unpaid order into the till rebuilt it from the product rather than the variation ordered, so the stock came off the wrong size. It now carries the variation through.
 Fixed

What is not done, stated plainly

We would rather you read these here than discover them at the counter. Nothing below is a surprise to us; each is either awaiting a decision from you or scheduled.

ItemWhat it means for youStatus
A saved invoice's items cannot be edited
To correct a billed item you must delete the invoice and enter it again, which uses up the invoice number. A paid invoice cannot be deleted at all — it has to be voided first. We want to agree with you which invoices should be editable (unpaid drafts only, or any unpaid invoice) before building this, because it decides how your numbering and your books behave.
 Needs your decision
The public website does not yet offer variations
Your own screens — the till, invoices, Collections — all sell by variation. The customer-facing shop still lists products only, so a web customer cannot pick a size or colour, and the order comes off your variations in listed order rather than the one they wanted. Everything needed for it is now in place; it is a screen-building job.
 Scheduled
Tailoring work does not deduct fabric
A custom garment records the fabric and its cost on the invoice, but the metres are not taken out of your fabric stock and nothing appears in the stock movement log. Fabric counts therefore only change when you adjust them by hand.
 Scheduled
Stock lost before the void/delete fix was not recovered
Invoices cancelled before that change did not return their stock, and we have not swept back through them — a blind correction could easily make counts worse rather than better. If you tell us a date range you trust, we can reconcile it with you.
 Worth knowing
Tax is stored as an amount, not a percentage
You type a rate when raising an invoice, but only the shilling amount is kept. A finished invoice can therefore never show the percentage it was worked out from, and there is no default rate — it must be typed each time. Worth fixing when invoice editing is built, since the two touch the same screen.
 Worth knowing
Searching by size works on Collections, not on Inventory
Typing 21.5 finds the product on the Collections screen, at the till and in the invoice picker. The separate Inventory screen still searches name and code only, so the same search there returns nothing.
 Worth knowing

Round one — your written review of 16 AugustAll resolved

The three findings, point by point

ISSUE 1

Inability to add or select a person

You rated: High Resolved
You reported"The Person field operates as a restricted dropdown without an in-line option or modal trigger to create/add a new person profile on the fly… Add an inline '+ Add New Person' button/option directly inside or beside the dropdown and implement a quick modal overlay."
  • Delivered exactly as requested — in both placements. An "Add New Person" action now sits beside the Person field, and a "＋ Add new person…" entry sits inside the dropdown itself.
  • A quick modal overlay captures the person's name, relationship, phone, gender and age group, and saves them under the invoice's client — the invoice in progress is untouched; nothing already entered is lost.
  • The new person is selected automatically on that line item the moment they are saved, so work continues without a single extra click.

How it behaves now: an invoice for a family of five who have never been recorded can be raised in one sitting — each person added inline as their garment line is created.

ISSUE 2

Fabric selection missing 'Ridhaa'

You rated: Medium Resolved
You reported"The Fabric dropdown defaults to 'No fabric' or fails to populate/display required fabric categories, specifically missing the 'Ridhaa' option requested by the client… ensure 'Ridhaa' and all active materials dynamically sync from the Fabrics management module."
  • 'Ridhaa' now exists in the fabric master catalogue as an active material and appears in the invoice fabric dropdown alongside the other eight fabrics. It ships to the live system as a safe, one-time database update.
  • The fabric list now re-syncs from the Fabrics module every time the Items step opens — a fabric added by a manager appears in the invoice screen immediately, with no page refresh and no re-deployment. This is the "dynamic sync" your review asked for.
  • Selecting a fabric still auto-fills its cost from the price per unit held in the Fabrics module, so pricing stays consistent between inventory and invoicing.

One input needed from you: Ridhaa is priced at a placeholder KES 1,200 per unit. Confirm the real price and it is a one-minute edit in the Fabrics module — no developer needed.

ISSUE 3

Measurement selection / assignment not working

You rated: High Resolved
You reported"The Measurement selector remains set to 'None' and fails to load existing measurement profiles or allow direct measurement entry… Fix conditional linking so selecting a Person populates their saved measurement profiles, and add an inline '+ Add/Edit Measurements' modal trigger."
  • Root cause found and fixed. Two gaps produced the behaviour your testers saw: a person with no saved measurements offered no way to create one from the invoice, and switching persons silently kept the previous person's measurement on the line. Both are corrected — changing the person now clears the stale selection, and the selector stays disabled until a person is chosen.
  • The inline "+ Add/Edit Measurements" trigger is in — both halves. Add opens a blank measurement form; Edit appears the moment a measurement is selected and opens that record pre-filled, so a wrong figure is corrected on the spot instead of abandoning the invoice to go to the person's file. Both sit beside the selector and inside the dropdown, and both offer garment type, label/occasion, date, a cm ⇄ inches toggle that converts values already typed, and the exact measurement fields configured per garment type in Settings (shoulder, chest, kanzu length, and so on).
  • The saved measurement links to the invoice line instantly, so the tailor receives accurate garment dimensions attached directly to the order — the outcome your review asked for.
  • A clear amber hint now appears when the selected person has no measurements yet, pointing staff to "Add Measurement" instead of leaving a silent "None".

How it behaves now: select a person → their saved profiles load by name and garment type; none on file → capture one in the modal without leaving the invoice; a figure that needs correcting → edit it in place. Either way it attaches to the line the moment it is saved.

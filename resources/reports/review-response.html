Round one — your written review of 16 AugustAll resolved

The three findings, point by point

ISSUE 1

Inability to add or select a person

You rated: High Resolved
You reported"The Person field operates as a restricted dropdown without an in-line option or modal trigger to create/add a new person profile on the fly… Add an inline '+ Add New Person' button/option directly inside or beside the dropdown and implement a quick modal overlay."
  • Delivered exactly as requested — in both placements. An "Add New Person" action now sits beside the Person field, and a "＋ Add new person…" entry sits inside the dropdown itself.
  • A quick modal overlay captures the person's name, relationship, phone, gender and age group, and saves them under the invoice's client — the invoice in progress is untouched; nothing already entered is lost.
  • The new person is selected automatically on that line item the moment they are saved, so work continues without a single extra click.

How it behaves now: an invoice for a family of five who have never been recorded can be raised in one sitting — each person added inline as their garment line is created.

ISSUE 2

Fabric selection missing 'Ridhaa'

You rated: Medium Resolved
You reported"The Fabric dropdown defaults to 'No fabric' or fails to populate/display required fabric categories, specifically missing the 'Ridhaa' option requested by the client… ensure 'Ridhaa' and all active materials dynamically sync from the Fabrics management module."
  • 'Ridhaa' now exists in the fabric master catalogue as an active material and appears in the invoice fabric dropdown alongside the other eight fabrics. It ships to the live system as a safe, one-time database update.
  • The fabric list now re-syncs from the Fabrics module every time the Items step opens — a fabric added by a manager appears in the invoice screen immediately, with no page refresh and no re-deployment. This is the "dynamic sync" your review asked for.
  • Selecting a fabric still auto-fills its cost from the price per unit held in the Fabrics module, so pricing stays consistent between inventory and invoicing.

One input needed from you: Ridhaa is priced at a placeholder KES 1,200 per unit. Confirm the real price and it is a one-minute edit in the Fabrics module — no developer needed.

ISSUE 3

Measurement selection / assignment not working

You rated: High Resolved
You reported"The Measurement selector remains set to 'None' and fails to load existing measurement profiles or allow direct measurement entry… Fix conditional linking so selecting a Person populates their saved measurement profiles, and add an inline '+ Add/Edit Measurements' modal trigger."
  • Root cause found and fixed. Two gaps produced the behaviour your testers saw: a person with no saved measurements offered no way to create one from the invoice, and switching persons silently kept the previous person's measurement on the line. Both are corrected — changing the person now clears the stale selection, and the selector stays disabled until a person is chosen.
  • The inline "+ Add/Edit Measurements" trigger is in — both halves. Add opens a blank measurement form; Edit appears the moment a measurement is selected and opens that record pre-filled, so a wrong figure is corrected on the spot instead of abandoning the invoice to go to the person's file. Both sit beside the selector and inside the dropdown, and both offer garment type, label/occasion, date, a cm ⇄ inches toggle that converts values already typed, and the exact measurement fields configured per garment type in Settings (shoulder, chest, kanzu length, and so on).
  • The saved measurement links to the invoice line instantly, so the tailor receives accurate garment dimensions attached directly to the order — the outcome your review asked for.
  • A clear amber hint now appears when the selected person has no measurements yet, pointing staff to "Add Measurement" instead of leaving a silent "None".

How it behaves now: select a person → their saved profiles load by name and garment type; none on file → capture one in the modal without leaving the invoice; a figure that needs correcting → edit it in place. Either way it attaches to the line the moment it is saved.
